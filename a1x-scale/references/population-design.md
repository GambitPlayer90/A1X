# Population design

## Sources of meaningful diversity

Diversify by:

- different protocol obligations or terminal consumers;
- producer/consumer mismatch versus lifecycle interruption;
- atomic, callback, and multi-transaction actor models;
- different asset/unit/domain/epoch interpretations;
- forward execution versus consumer-backward reasoning;
- manual semantic composition versus fuzz or symbolic search;
- strongest-refutation lane versus exploit-construction lane.

Do not assign multiple lanes the same prompt, files, and hypothesis and call their stochastic wording diversity.

## Lane handoff

Require:

- exact target identity and starting state;
- hypothesis and obligation IDs;
- code/deployment facts used;
- replayable prefix and commands;
- observations versus interpretations;
- evidence level;
- blocker and next discriminator;
- refuting evidence and limitations.

## Selection

Deduplicate only exact artifact-bound, ordered-sequence identities. Reserve one active elite per coarse root-cause family before global Pareto ranking. A reachable low-impact contradiction and an unproven high-impact mechanism may both stay until a discriminating test resolves them. Put capacity overflow in the persistent recombination reserve; never label dominance as refutation. Inject fresh lanes after convergence.

## Stop conditions

Stop a round when new lanes/recombinations produce no new mechanism, state, constraint resolution, or evidence improvement; execution cost rises without information gain; or remaining candidates are blocked by the same external dependency. Do not apply stagnation stopping while a protected high-consequence or high-novelty lead has an unresolved discriminator. Record marginal gains instead of assuming more compute is monotonic.
